Don Mclaire

I'm Don Mclaire C. Gonzales, a third-year BS Information Technology student at Batangas State University – The National Engineering University, CICS Alangilan.

I enjoy noticing what could work better, understanding the people affected, and exploring what a practical solution might look like. My projects span educational platforms, campus safety, infrastructure transparency, and agricultural automation.

How I build

I use AI-assisted programming as part of my development process. BasCal, SafeTap, PITS, and the HyperLink challenges were solo development efforts with AI assistance. Silong Hamuros is a team project, with my role focused on coordination and application feature planning.

Why I build

I don't need to have every answer before I start. I need a worthwhile problem, the willingness to learn, and the patience to keep refining an idea until it becomes something tangible.
